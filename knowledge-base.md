# TALA Knowledge Base — BAIA, Marina Terrace, San Vicente, Mergato

## About the Resorts

### BAIA (Barangay Bucana, Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro)
- **Location**: Barangay Bucana, Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro, Philippines
- **Type**: Boutique beach resort with rooms and villas
- **Beach**: Semi-private white sand beach, calm waters, mountain backdrop
- **Rooms**: Ocean-view rooms, garden rooms, family suites
- **Amenities**: Infinity pool, restaurant (Filipino + international), beach bar, spa, free WiFi, airport/station pickup
- **Activities**: Island hopping, snorkeling, diving, kayaking, trekking, ATV rides
- **Vibe**: Relaxed luxury, romantic, family-friendly, Instagram-worthy
- **Distance**: ~3 hours from Manila (batangas port ferry)
- **Best for**: Couples, families, solo travelers, digital nomads

### Marina Terrace (San Vicente, Palawan)
- **Location**: San Vicente, Palawan, Philippines
- **Type**: Beachfront villa resort
- **Beach**: Long stretch of white sand, clear water, sunset views
- **Villas**: Private villas with outdoor showers, aircon, terraces
- **Amenities**: Restaurant, bar, infinity pool, massage, free WiFi, airport pickup
- **Activities**: Island hopping, beach volleyball, stand-up paddleboard, sunset cruise
- **Vibe**: Quiet, intimate, tropical paradise, off-the-beaten-path
- **Distance**: ~2.5 hours from Puerto Princesa airport
- **Best for**: Honeymooners, couples, travelers seeking peace

## San Vicente, Palawan (Marina Terrace location)
- **Airport**: San Vicente Airport (VXV) — direct flights from Manila, Cebu, Clark
- **Long Beach**: 14.7 km white sand beach — longest in Palawan
- **Nearby islands**: Boayah, Matalang, Exotic Island
- **Tourist season**: November to May (dry season)
- **Getting there**: 2.5 hrs from Puerto Princesa airport by van

## Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro (BAIA location)
- **Port**: Batangas City (3 hrs from Manila by bus/jeepney)
- **Ferry**: 1.5 hrs to White Beach or Sabang, then tricycle to Bucana
- **Famous for**: Diving (UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve), beaches, nightlife in Sabang
- **Tourist season**: November to May
- **Nearby**: Tamaraw Falls, Mangyan communities, Mindoro wildlife
